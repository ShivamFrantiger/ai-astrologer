# Fundamentals of Vedic Astrology (Jyotish Shastra)

**Source:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), Chapters 1–3
**Author:** Sage Parashara
**Category:** general_vedic
**Classical Authority:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Parashari Tradition

## Core Philosophy of Karma and Planetary Influence
In Vedic astrology (Jyotish), the horoscope (Kundli) is not a deterministic or fatalistic prediction of an unchangeable fate. Rather, it is a map of Prarabdha Karma—the portion of accumulated past karmas designated to bear fruit in the current lifetime. 
Vedic philosophy identifies three layers of karma:
1. **Sanchita Karma:** The vast storehouse of all accumulated past actions.
2. **Prarabdha Karma:** The specific subset active in this lifetime, indicated by planetary positions at birth.
3. **Kriyamana Karma:** Current free will and action in the present moment, which has the power to modify, alleviate, or overcome planetary obstacles.

Planets (Grahas) do not cause events mechanically. In classical Sanskrit, "Graha" literally means "that which seizes or reflects". The planets act as cosmic reflectors or karmic deliverers (Karmaphala Daata), indicating the timing and nature of karmic harvests.

## The Pillars of a Horoscope
Every Vedic birth chart is constructed on four foundational pillars:
1. **Lagna (Ascendant):** The zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the precise time and place of birth. The Lagna represents physical vitality, personality, temperament, head, and the native's general orientation toward life.
2. **Chandra Rashi (Moon Sign):** The sign occupied by the Moon at birth. The Moon rules the mind (Manas), emotional stability, subconscious instincts, and perception of reality. In Vedic astrology, the Moon sign is equally as important as the Ascendant for psychological assessment and transit evaluations (Gochar).
3. **Surya (Sun):** The soul (Atman), core dignity, self-confidence, father, authority, and life force.
4. **Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions):** The 27-fold division of the zodiac that gives nuanced psychological traits, deity archetypes, and governs the Vimshottari Dasha timing system.

## The Zodiac and Sidereal Calculations (Nirayana System)
Vedic astrology uses the Sidereal (Nirayana) zodiac, which is anchored to the fixed stars and accounts for the precession of the equinoxes (Ayanamsha, typically Lahiri/Chitra Paksha). This differs fundamentally from the Tropical (Sayana) zodiac used in modern Western astrology.

## The Four Purusharthas (Goals of Human Life)
The 12 houses of the horoscope correspond to the four legitimate aims of human existence:
- **Dharma (Righteousness & Purpose):** Houses 1, 5, 9 (Trikona houses) represent personal ethics, intellect, spiritual merit, and destiny.
- **Artha (Wealth & Resources):** Houses 2, 6, 10 represent tangible possessions, daily livelihood, and professional standing.
- **Kama (Desire & Relationships):** Houses 3, 7, 11 represent courage, relationships, social networks, and fulfillment of hopes.
- **Moksha (Liberation & Transcendence):** Houses 4, 8, 12 represent inner emotional peace, transformation, occult wisdom, and spiritual detachment.
