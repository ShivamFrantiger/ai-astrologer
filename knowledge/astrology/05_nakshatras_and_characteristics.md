# The 27 Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions)

**Source:** Taittiriya Brahmana, Brihat Samhita, and Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
**Author:** Sage Parashara & Varahamihira
**Category:** nakshatras
**Classical Authority:** Classical Vedic Nakshatra System

## Nakshatra Architecture
The zodiac circle of 360° is divided into 27 Nakshatras of 13°20' (800 arcminutes) each.
Each Nakshatra is further subdivided into 4 Padas (quarters) of 3°20' (200 arcminutes). 
27 Nakshatras × 4 Padas = 108 Padas, which maps exactly to the 108 Navamshas of the D9 divisional chart.

The Moon's Nakshatra at the moment of birth (Janma Nakshatra) determines:
1. The native's innate mental temperament and subconscious archetype.
2. The initial running Mahadasha in the 120-year Vimshottari cycle.
3. The Ashtakoota compatibility score in Kundli Milan.
