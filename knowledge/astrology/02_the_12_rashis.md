# The 12 Signs of the Zodiac (Rashis)

**Source:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapter 4 & Phaladeepika, Chapter 1
**Author:** Sage Parashara & Mantreswara
**Category:** rashis
**Classical Authority:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

## Classification of Rashis
The 12 signs of the sidereal zodiac are categorized by element, mobility, gender, and nature:
- **Fire Signs (Agni Tatva):** Aries (Mesha), Leo (Simha), Sagittarius (Dhanu). High energy, ambition, optimism, courage, leadership, and spontaneous action.
- **Earth Signs (Prithvi Tatva):** Taurus (Vrishabha), Virgo (Kanya), Capricorn (Makara). Practicality, patience, physical stamina, financial prudence, reliability, and methodical focus.
- **Air Signs (Vayu Tatva):** Gemini (Mithuna), Libra (Tula), Aquarius (Kumbha). Intellect, communication, social adaptability, philosophy, ideation, and networking.
- **Water Signs (Jala Tatva):** Cancer (Karka), Scorpio (Vrischika), Pisces (Meena). Emotional depth, intuition, empathy, imagination, spiritual sensitivity, and protective nature.

### Mobility of Signs
- **Movable (Chara):** Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Initiators, enterprising, adaptable, dynamic, quick to begin new endeavors.
- **Fixed (Sthira):** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Stable, resolute, enduring, steadfast, resistant to sudden disruption, patient accumulators.
- **Dual / Common (Dwiswabhava):** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces. Analytical, flexible, intellectually balanced, versatile, able to navigate transition.

## Detailed Significance of Each Rashi

### 1. Mesha (Aries) - 0° to 30°
- **Lord:** Mars (Mangal). **Element:** Fire (Agni). **Nature:** Movable, Male.
- **Symbol:** Ram. **Exaltation of:** Sun (Surya at 10°). **Debilitation of:** Saturn (Shani at 20°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Pioneering spirit, courage, competitive drive, initiative, direct communication, independence. Vulnerable to impatience, impulsiveness, and headstrong anger.
- **Body Part:** Head, brain, facial bones.

### 2. Vrishabha (Taurus) - 30° to 60°
- **Lord:** Venus (Shukra). **Element:** Earth (Prithvi). **Nature:** Fixed, Female.
- **Symbol:** Bull. **Exaltation of:** Moon (Chandra at 3°). **Debilitation of:** Ketu (traditionally).
- **Key Characteristics:** Stability, aesthetic appreciation, determination, loyalty, financial accumulation, culinary interest, endurance. Vulnerable to stubbornness and reluctance to change.
- **Body Part:** Face, throat, neck, vocal cords.

### 3. Mithuna (Gemini) - 60° to 90°
- **Lord:** Mercury (Budha). **Element:** Air (Vayu). **Nature:** Dual, Male.
- **Symbol:** Twins holding a mace and lute.
- **Key Characteristics:** Curiosity, communication skills, wit, adaptability, business acumen, trade, multiple interests. Vulnerable to scattered focus and restlessness.
- **Body Part:** Arms, shoulders, respiratory tract, nervous system.

### 4. Karka (Cancer) - 90° to 120°
- **Lord:** Moon (Chandra). **Element:** Water (Jala). **Nature:** Movable, Female.
- **Symbol:** Crab. **Exaltation of:** Jupiter (Guru at 5°). **Debilitation of:** Mars (Mangal at 28°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Deep nurturing instinct, protective emotional bonds, memory, devotion to family, intuitive empathy. Vulnerable to mood fluctuations and defensiveness.
- **Body Part:** Chest, lungs, breasts, stomach.

### 5. Simha (Leo) - 120° to 150°
- **Lord:** Sun (Surya). **Element:** Fire (Agni). **Nature:** Fixed, Male.
- **Symbol:** Lion.
- **Key Characteristics:** Natural authority, magnanimity, regal confidence, creative expression, leadership, loyalty, strong sense of honor. Vulnerable to pride and need for validation.
- **Body Part:** Heart, spine, upper back.

### 6. Kanya (Virgo) - 150° to 180°
- **Lord:** Mercury (Budha). **Element:** Earth (Prithvi). **Nature:** Dual, Female.
- **Symbol:** Virgin in a boat with grain and fire. **Exaltation of:** Mercury (at 15°). **Debilitation of:** Venus (Shukra at 27°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Analytical precision, service orientation, discernment, hygiene, skill in craft, problem solving, healing. Vulnerable to over-criticism and anxiety.
- **Body Part:** Abdomen, digestive organs, intestines.

### 7. Tula (Libra) - 180° to 210°
- **Lord:** Venus (Shukra). **Element:** Air (Vayu). **Nature:** Movable, Male.
- **Symbol:** Scales of balance. **Exaltation of:** Saturn (Shani at 20°). **Debilitation of:** Sun (Surya at 10°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Diplomacy, justice, partnership orientation, aesthetic sense, commercial tact, fairness, social charm. Vulnerable to indecision and conflict avoidance.
- **Body Part:** Lower back, kidneys, lumbar region.

### 8. Vrischika (Scorpio) - 210° to 240°
- **Lord:** Mars (Mangal) & Ketu (co-ruler). **Element:** Water (Jala). **Nature:** Fixed, Female.
- **Symbol:** Scorpion. **Debilitation of:** Moon (Chandra at 3°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Intensity, resilience, investigative depth, psychological insight, transformative capability, loyalty, discretion. Vulnerable to secrecy, grudge-holding, and jealousy.
- **Body Part:** Generative organs, excretory system, pelvis.

### 9. Dhanu (Sagittarius) - 240° to 270°
- **Lord:** Jupiter (Guru). **Element:** Fire (Agni). **Nature:** Dual, Male.
- **Symbol:** Centaur archer. **Exaltation of:** Ketu (traditionally). **Debilitation of:** Rahu (traditionally).
- **Key Characteristics:** Philosophical aspiration, optimism, thirst for higher knowledge, righteousness (Dharma), enthusiasm, generosity. Vulnerable to dogmatism and bluntness.
- **Body Part:** Thighs, hips, arterial system.

### 10. Makara (Capricorn) - 270° to 300°
- **Lord:** Saturn (Shani). **Element:** Earth (Prithvi). **Nature:** Movable, Female.
- **Symbol:** Sea-monster / Crocodile-goat (Makara). **Exaltation of:** Mars (Mangal at 28°). **Debilitation of:** Jupiter (Guru at 5°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Pragmatic ambition, discipline, long-term perseverance, organizational skill, duty, respect for tradition. Vulnerable to pessimism, emotional stiffness, and workaholism.
- **Body Part:** Knees, joints, skeletal framework.

### 11. Kumbha (Aquarius) - 300° to 330°
- **Lord:** Saturn (Shani) & Rahu (co-ruler). **Element:** Air (Vayu). **Nature:** Fixed, Male.
- **Symbol:** Water-bearer pouring pitcher.
- **Key Characteristics:** Egalitarianism, vision for society, scientific thinking, originality, humanitarian ideals, large network circles. Vulnerable to detachment and unconventional stubbornness.
- **Body Part:** Calves, shins, ankles, circulation.

### 12. Meena (Pisces) - 330° to 360°
- **Lord:** Jupiter (Guru). **Element:** Water (Jala). **Nature:** Dual, Female.
- **Symbol:** Two fish swimming in opposite directions. **Exaltation of:** Venus (Shukra at 27°). **Debilitation of:** Mercury (Budha at 15°).
- **Key Characteristics:** Compassion, spiritual inclination, artistic imagination, empathy, transcendence, sacrifice, universal love. Vulnerable to escapism, lack of boundaries, and dreaminess.
- **Body Part:** Feet, toes, lymphatic system.
