# Marriage Compatibility (Kundli Milan) & Relationship Astrology

**Source:** Muhurtha Chintamani, Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapter 20; Phaladeepika, Chapter 10
**Author:** Sage Parashara, Mantreswara, Rama Daivajna
**Category:** marriage_compatibility
**Classical Authority:** Classical Ashtakoota Milan System

## Foundations of Relationship Astrology
In Vedic astrology, marriage analysis is never reduced to Sun signs or simple matching. It evaluates three distinct layers:
1. **The Individual Chart's Capacity for Partnership:** Strength of the 7th house, 7th lord, Venus (Kalatrakaraka for all/men), Jupiter (Dharakaraka for women), and the 2nd house (family life).
2. **Ashtakoota Guna Milan (The 36-Guna System):** Systematic evaluation of the two Janma Nakshatras and Moon signs for psycho-physical resonance.
3. **Navamsha Chart (D9):** The harmonic chart reflecting the soul's inner marital destiny and the second half of life.

## The Ashtakoota 36-Point Compatibility System
The 36 points are distributed across eight distinct psycho-spiritual categories:

1. **Varna (1 Point) - Spiritual Temperament:**
   - Evaluates ego harmony and spiritual orientation.
2. **Vashya (2 Points) - Mutual Magnetic Attraction:**
   - Evaluates natural psychological dominance and attraction between signs.
3. **Tara (3 Points) - Destiny & Longevity (Health):**
   - Evaluates the auspiciousness of birth stars based on the 9 Tara cycles.
4. **Yoni (4 Points) - Biological & Physical Compatibility:**
   - Based on animal archetypes of Nakshatras; ensures instinctual harmony and intimacy comfort.
5. **Graha Maitri (5 Points) - Mental Friendship of Moon Lords:**
   - Examines whether the lords of the two Moon signs are natural friends, neutral, or enemies. Critical for everyday domestic communication and intellectual rapport.
6. **Gana (6 Points) - Temperament & Core Values:**
   - Classifies Nakshatras into Deva (divine/gentle), Manushya (human/balanced), and Rakshasa (fiery/assertive). Evaluates basic life attitudes and conflict styles.
7. **Bhakoot (7 Points) - Emotional Resonance & Family Welfare:**
   - Evaluates the relative distance between the two Moon signs (e.g. 6-8 Shadashtaka, 9-5 Navapanchama, 7-7 Saptama). Bhakoot Dosha indicates emotional disconnect or financial stress, but has numerous classical cancellations (such as common lord or friendly lords).
8. **Nadi (8 Points) - Health, Genetic Harmony & Spiritual Union:**
   - Divided into Vata (Adi), Pitta (Madhya), and Kapha (Antya). Ensures complementary physiological balance and healthy lineage. Nadi Dosha carries the highest weight (8 points).

### Scoring Guidelines:
- **Below 18 Points:** Low traditional compatibility; requires careful conscious communication, mutual compromise, and individual chart strength.
- **18 to 24 Points:** Moderate, average compatibility; common and manageable with good mutual understanding.
- **25 to 32 Points:** High compatibility; harmonious natural alignment across mind, emotions, and practical life.
- **Above 32 Points:** Exceptional alignment; rare and deeply supportive partnership.

## 7th House and Timing of Marriage
- **Favorable Factors for Marriage:** 
  - Connection between the 7th lord and 1st, 2nd, 5th, 9th, or 11th lords.
  - Benefic transits of Jupiter aspecting the 7th house, 7th lord, or natal Venus.
  - Activation of Mahadasha or Antardasha of the 7th lord, Venus, or planets situated in the 7th house.
- **Factors Indicating Delay (Vivah Vilamb):**
  - Saturn aspecting or occupying the 7th house/lord (delays marriage usually past age 27–30 to teach maturity).
  - Retrograde 7th lord or combust Venus/Jupiter.
  - Rahu or Ketu in the 1-7 axis creating unconventional marital expectations.
  - Delay is NOT denial: Saturn-influenced 7th houses often produce stable, long-lasting unions when entered with mature discernment.

## Ethical Boundaries in Marriage Counseling
1. **No Fatalistic Proclamations:** Never claim that a marriage is "doomed" or "guaranteed to end in divorce". Astrology reveals behavioral tendencies, which conscious humans can actively navigate.
2. **Kuja Dosha Balance:** Always check for classical cancellations before labeling someone Manglik.
3. **Mutual Respect Over Scores:** Traditional texts emphasize that love, character (Sheela), and mutual respect take precedence over mathematical point totals alone.
