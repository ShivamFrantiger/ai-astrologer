# Vimshottari Dasha Timing System and Transits (Gochar)

**Source:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapters 46–48; Phaladeepika, Chapter 20
**Author:** Sage Parashara & Mantreswara
**Category:** dasha_timing
**Classical Authority:** Parashari Vimshottari Dasha System

## The 120-Year Vimshottari Cycle
The Vimshottari Dasha is the premier predictive tool in Vedic astrology, tailored for the human lifespan of 120 years. 
The starting dasha is determined strictly by the Moon's exact longitude within its Janma Nakshatra at the moment of birth.

### Sequence and Duration of Mahadashas:
1. **Ketu Mahadasha:** 7 Years. Detachment, spiritual introspection, unexpected shifts, past-life karma.
2. **Shukra (Venus) Mahadasha:** 20 Years. Material pleasures, relationships, marriage, creative arts, vehicles, luxury.
3. **Surya (Sun) Mahadasha:** 6 Years. Career authority, government interactions, self-realization, father-related events.
4. **Chandra (Moon) Mahadasha:** 10 Years. Emotional maturation, domestic shifts, public popularity, nurturing projects.
5. **Mangal (Mars) Mahadasha:** 7 Years. High physical drive, land/property acquisitions, competitive focus, courage.
6. **Rahu Mahadasha:** 18 Years. Unconventional expansion, ambition, foreign travel, technological pursuit, sudden swings.
7. **Guru (Jupiter) Mahadasha:** 16 Years. Scholarly wisdom, spiritual mentorship, birth of children, financial stability, Dharma.
8. **Shani (Saturn) Mahadasha:** 19 Years. Discipline, patience, hard labor, mass organizing, humility, enduring foundation building.
9. **Budha (Mercury) Mahadasha:** 17 Years. Business enterprise, analytical study, communication, writing, financial networking.

## Classical Principles of Dasha Interpretation

### 1. The Role of the Mahadasha Lord
The Mahadasha lord sets the macro environment and overall life theme. Its results are evaluated by:
- The houses it owns in the natal chart (Lordship).
- The house in which it is placed (Placement).
- The planets it is conjunct with or aspected by.
- Its dignity (exalted, own sign, friend's sign, debilitated, combust).

### 2. The Role of the Antardasha Lord (Sub-Period)
The Antardasha lord acts as the executor that triggers specific events. 
- If the Mahadasha lord and Antardasha lord are in a **harmonious relationship (1-5-9 or 3-11)** from each other, the period yields smooth progress and positive results.
- If the Mahadasha and Antardasha lords are in a **challenging relationship (6-8 or 2-12 Shadashtaka / Dwirdwadasha)** from each other, the period brings internal friction, disputes, sudden expenses, or health challenges, even if both planets are intrinsically benefic.

### 3. Planetary Maturity Ages (Naisargika Vayas)
Planets naturally ripen and deliver their full conscious fruits at specific biological ages:
- **Jupiter:** Age 16 (Awakening of higher intellect).
- **Sun:** Age 21–22 (Awakening of self-identity and independent dignity).
- **Moon:** Age 23–24 (Emotional independence and maturity).
- **Venus:** Age 25 (Romantic clarity and artistic refinement).
- **Mars:** Age 28 (Focus of physical drive and temperamental stability).
- **Mercury:** Age 32 (Full commercial discernment and business prudence).
- **Saturn:** Age 36 (Deep endurance, professional grounding, karmic payoff).
- **Rahu:** Age 42 (Realization of worldly ambition and disillusionment with superficial desires).
- **Ketu:** Age 48 (Spiritual wisdom and philosophical surrender).

## The Interplay of Dasha and Gochar (Transits)
Dasha represents the internal karmic clock, while Gochar (planetary transit) provides the external trigger.
- **Dasha is Primary:** A favorable transit cannot deliver major results unless the running Mahadasha/Antardasha supports that life area.
- **Transit of Slow Planets:** Saturn (2.5 years per sign) and Jupiter (1 year per sign) are the key cosmic timekeepers:
  - **Sade Sati:** The 7.5-year transit of Saturn through the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from the natal Moon. It demands psychological cleansing, self-reliance, and eradication of illusions.
  - **Jupiter's Transit:** When transiting Jupiter aspects natal houses or planets, it expands opportunities, grants protection, and facilitates auspicious events like marriage, childbirth, or educational milestones.
