# Planetary Aspects, Conjunctions, Yogas, and Doshas

**Source:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapters 35–42; Phaladeepika, Chapters 6 & 7
**Author:** Sage Parashara & Mantreswara
**Category:** yogas_doshas
**Classical Authority:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

## Classical Planetary Aspects (Drishti)
In Vedic astrology, planetary gaze (Drishti) transfers the planet's nature and agenda to another house or planet:
1. **Universal Aspect:** All planets cast a full 100% aspect on the 7th house (180°) from their position.
2. **Special Aspects (Vishesha Drishti):**
   - **Mars (Mangal):** Casts full aspect on the 4th, 7th, and 8th houses from its placement. Mars exerts protective, energetic, or abrasive pressure on these points.
   - **Jupiter (Guru):** Casts full aspect on the 5th, 7th, and 9th houses from its placement. Jupiter's aspect is known as Amrita Drishti (nectar gaze), providing grace, wisdom, and neutralizing afflictions.
   - **Saturn (Shani):** Casts full aspect on the 3rd, 7th, and 10th houses from its placement. Saturn's aspect introduces discipline, delay, sobriety, and long-term testing.
   - **Rahu & Ketu:** Traditional Parashari texts accord Rahu and Ketu 5th and 9th trinal aspects, impacting subtle subconscious faculties.

## Auspicious Yogas (Planetary Combinations)

### 1. Raja Yogas (Combinations for Status & Power)
- **Principle:** Formed by the mutual relationship (conjunction, mutual aspect, or parivartana/mutual exchange) between a lord of a Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10 - Vishnu Sthanas) and a lord of a Trikona (1, 5, 9 - Lakshmi Sthanas).
- **Effect:** Elevates the native in social standing, career authority, respect, and influence during the running dashas of the participating planets.

### 2. Dhana Yogas (Combinations for Wealth)
- **Principle:** Mutual associations between the 1st lord (self), 2nd lord (accumulated wealth), 5th lord (Purva Punya/speculation), 9th lord (fortune/Bhagya), and 11th lord (gains/Labha).
- **Effect:** Facilitates financial abundance, successful business ventures, and reliable capital accumulation.

### 3. Gajakesari Yoga
- **Condition:** Jupiter occupies a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th) from the natal Moon, and Jupiter is not heavily afflicted by Saturn or Rahu.
- **Effect:** Bestows intellectual reputation, noble temperament, enduring public respect, eloquence, and lasting prosperity.

### 4. Budhaditya Yoga
- **Condition:** Sun and Mercury are conjunct in the same house (preferably 1st, 10th, 11th, 5th, or 9th) without severe combustion.
- **Effect:** Gives sharp analytical intelligence, mathematical prowess, administrative skill, and success in scholarly or commercial professions.

### 5. Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (The Five Great Person Yogas)
Formed when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn occupies a Kendra house (1, 4, 7, 10) in its own sign or exaltation sign:
1. **Ruchaka Yoga (Mars):** Physical vigor, courage, military or engineering leadership, unyielding fortitude.
2. **Bhadra Yoga (Mercury):** Intellectual brilliance, commercial genius, eloquent speech, longevity, scholarly renown.
3. **Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter):** Spiritual wisdom, religious devotion, universal benevolence, high ethical standing.
4. **Malavya Yoga (Venus):** Artistic mastery, immense sensory luxury, vehicles, magnetic charm, happy marital comforts.
5. **Sasa Yoga (Saturn):** Mass leadership, political influence through the working class, resilience, organizational command.

### 6. Vipareeta Raja Yogas (Success Through Overcoming Adversity)
Formed when lords of Dusthanas (6th, 8th, or 12th) occupy other Dusthanas, without association with benefic Kendra/Trikona lords:
- **Harsha Yoga (6th lord in 6, 8, 12):** Defeat of rivals, freedom from severe diseases, physical vitality.
- **Sarala Yoga (8th lord in 6, 8, 12):** Longevity, fearlessness, sudden unexpected windfalls, resilience in crisis.
- **Vimala Yoga (12th lord in 6, 8, 12):** Frugality, righteous independence, peaceful retirement, spiritual contentment.

## Astrological Doshas and Their Classical Reality

### 1. Manglik Dosha (Kuja Dosha)
- **Definition:** Occurs when Mars is positioned in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from the Lagna, Moon, or Venus.
- **Traditional Meaning:** Represents high fire and martial energy directed toward relationship domains, which can manifest as temperament mismatches, impatience, or tension in marital life if not constructively channeled.
- **Classical Cancellations (Neechabhanga & Mitigations):**
  - Mars in its own signs (Aries/Scorpio) or exaltation sign (Capricorn).
  - Mars aspected by or conjunct Jupiter or benefic Moon.
  - Mars in the 7th house in Cancer or Capricorn.
  - The partner also has a matching Kuja Dosha, creating energetic equilibrium.
  - Maturity: As the native passes age 28–30, raw Mars impulses naturally mature.

### 2. Kaal Sarp Yoga / Dosha
- **Definition:** Occurs when all seven physical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) are positioned within the arc between Rahu and Ketu.
- **Classical Context:** While popularized in modern times, classical principles categorize it as an intense karmic axis that creates concentrated early-life struggles followed by extraordinary delayed breakthroughs around age 36–42.
- **Constructive View:** Many great leaders and innovators possess this pattern; it provides intense relentless focus rather than mere misfortune.

### 3. Kemadruma Yoga
- **Definition:** Occurs when there are no physical planets (excluding Sun, Rahu, Ketu) in the 2nd and 12th houses from the natal Moon.
- **Traditional Meaning:** Indicates feelings of emotional loneliness, periods of financial fluctuation, or psychological isolation.
- **Cancellations:** If planets are in Kendras from the Lagna or Moon, Kemadruma is dissolved and converted into a resilient self-made success pattern.

### 4. Guru Chandal Yoga
- **Definition:** Conjunction of Jupiter and Rahu in the same sign.
- **Meaning:** Indicates an unconventional approach to religion, questioning traditional orthodoxies, and potential intellectual confusion if afflicted, but breakthrough innovation in philosophy when well-channeled.
