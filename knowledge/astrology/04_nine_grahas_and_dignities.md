# The Nine Planets (Navagrahas) and Their Dignities

**Source:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Chapters 3 & 45; Phaladeepika, Chapter 2
**Author:** Sage Parashara & Mantreswara
**Category:** grahas
**Classical Authority:** Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

## Classification of the Grahas
1. **Natural Benefics (Saumya Grahas):** Jupiter (Guru), Venus (Shukra), Waxing Moon (Shukla Paksha Chandra), and unafflicted Mercury (Budha). They promote harmony, wisdom, peace, prosperity, and gentle growth.
2. **Natural Malefics (Kroora Grahas):** Saturn (Shani), Mars (Mangal), Sun (Surya - technically mild/Krura), Rahu, Ketu, and Waning Moon (Krishna Paksha Chandra when very close to Sun). They introduce pressure, discipline, separation, acute passion, and endurance tests.

---

## Detailed Profile of Each Graha

### 1. Surya (The Sun) - Atmakaraka (Soul)
- **Significations:** Soul, ego, vitality, biological father, king, government, political power, courage, physician, gold, copper, forests, places of worship.
- **Ruler of:** Simha (Leo).
- **Exaltation:** Mesha (Aries) at 10°. **Debilitation:** Tula (Libra) at 10°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Simha 0° to 20°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 10th House (South / Noon).
- **Special Aspect:** Casts full 7th aspect.
- **Affliction / Combustion:** Planets within 6°–14° of the Sun become combust (Asta), causing their outer physical expressions to weaken while internalizing spiritual themes.

### 2. Chandra (The Moon) - Manakaraka (Mind)
- **Significations:** Mind (Manas), emotions, mother, liquid nourishment, blood circulation, public popularity, intuition, memory, silver, dairy, water bodies.
- **Ruler of:** Karka (Cancer).
- **Exaltation:** Vrishabha (Taurus) at 3°. **Debilitation:** Vrischika (Scorpio) at 3°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Vrishabha 4° to 30°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 4th House (North / Midnight).
- **Waxing vs Waning (Paksha Bala):** The Moon gains maximum strength (Paksha Bala) at Purnima (Full Moon) when 180° away from the Sun, and is weakest at Amavasya (New Moon).

### 3. Mangal (Mars) - Bhratrikaraka & Senapati (Commander)
- **Significations:** Energy, courage, initiative, younger brother, land/real estate (Bhoomi), engineering, military, police, surgery, martial arts, blood, weapons, fire.
- **Ruler of:** Mesha (Aries) and Vrischika (Scorpio).
- **Exaltation:** Makara (Capricorn) at 28°. **Debilitation:** Karka (Cancer) at 28°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Mesha 0° to 12°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 10th House (South).
- **Special Aspects (Vishesha Drishti):** 4th, 7th, and 8th houses from its placement.

### 4. Budha (Mercury) - Kumar / Buddhi (Intellect)
- **Significations:** Intellect, speech, logic, mathematics, trade, accounting, publishing, humor, astrology, nervous system, skin, green emerald, maternal relatives.
- **Ruler of:** Mithuna (Gemini) and Kanya (Virgo).
- **Exaltation:** Kanya (Virgo) at 15°. **Debilitation:** Meena (Pisces) at 15°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Kanya 16° to 20°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 1st House (East / Sunrise).
- **Chameleon Nature:** Mercury adopts the nature of any planet it is conjunct with; becomes malefic with malefics and benefic with benefics.

### 5. Guru / Brihaspati (Jupiter) - Mantri (Counselor & Priest)
- **Significations:** Wisdom (Jnana), divine grace, children (Putra), wealth (Dhana), Dharma, Guru/teacher, law, banking, philosophy, liver, fat tissue, yellow sapphire.
- **Ruler of:** Dhanu (Sagittarius) and Meena (Pisces).
- **Exaltation:** Karka (Cancer) at 5°. **Debilitation:** Makara (Capricorn) at 5°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Dhanu 0° to 10°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 1st House (East).
- **Special Aspects (Vishesha Drishti):** 5th, 7th, and 9th houses from its position. Jupiter's aspect is considered nectar (Amrita Drishti), neutralizing major chart afflictions.

### 6. Shukra (Venus) - Kalatrakaraka (Love & Beauty)
- **Significations:** Spouse, romantic love, sexual vitality, marriage, fine arts, music, luxury, vehicles, perfumes, diamond, reproductive fluids, kidneys.
- **Ruler of:** Vrishabha (Taurus) and Tula (Libra).
- **Exaltation:** Meena (Pisces) at 27°. **Debilitation:** Kanya (Virgo) at 27°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Tula 0° to 15°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 4th House (North).
- **Special Aspect:** Casts full 7th aspect.

### 7. Shani (Saturn) - Ayushkaraka (Longevity & Karma)
- **Significations:** Karma, longevity, discipline, delays, obstacles, perseverance, hard labor, blue collar workers, democratic masses, iron, minerals, nervous chronic ailments, old age.
- **Ruler of:** Makara (Capricorn) and Kumbha (Aquarius).
- **Exaltation:** Tula (Libra) at 20°. **Debilitation:** Mesha (Aries) at 20°.
- **Moolatrikona:** Kumbha 0° to 20°.
- **Directional Strength (Digbala):** 7th House (West / Sunset).
- **Special Aspects (Vishesha Drishti):** 3rd, 7th, and 10th houses from its placement. Saturn's gaze slows things down, demanding rigorous karmic accountability.

### 8. Rahu (North Lunar Node) - The Head of the Dragon
- **Significations:** Ambition, insatiable desire, innovation, foreign lands, unorthodox paths, technology, aviation, sudden wealth, illusions (Maya), poison, outcasts.
- **Exaltation:** Vrishabha (Taurus) / Mithuna (Gemini). **Debilitation:** Vrischika (Scorpio) / Dhanu (Sagittarius).
- **Friendly Signs:** Gemini, Virgo, Libra, Aquarius.
- **Special Aspects:** Traditional schools accord Rahu 5th, 7th, and 9th aspects like Jupiter.

### 9. Ketu (South Lunar Node) - The Tail of the Dragon / Mokshakaraka
- **Significations:** Spiritual liberation (Moksha), detachment (Vairagya), occult wisdom, asceticism, sudden separations, mathematics, psychic perception, past-life skills.
- **Exaltation:** Vrischika (Scorpio) / Dhanu (Sagittarius). **Debilitation:** Vrishabha (Taurus) / Mithuna (Gemini).
- **Nature:** Fiery, similar to Mars ("Kujavad Ketu, Shanivad Rahu").
